PART 3: CHECKPOINT TABLE & RESTART SAFETY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Start locald:

  $ ./target/release/edr_locald --telemetry-root $EDR_TELEMETRY_ROOT &
  $ sleep 2

Verify checkpoint table:

  $ sqlite3 $EDR_TELEMETRY_ROOT/analysis.db ".tables"

Expected:
  locald_checkpoint  telemetry_events  incidents

Check idempotency:

  $ BEFORE=$(sqlite3 $EDR_TELEMETRY_ROOT/analysis.db "SELECT COUNT(*) FROM telemetry_events;")
  $ echo "Events before restart: $BEFORE"
  $ pkill -f "edr_locald"
  $ sleep 1
  $ ./target/release/edr_locald --telemetry-root $EDR_TELEMETRY_ROOT &
  $ sleep 2
  $ AFTER=$(sqlite3 $EDR_TELEMETRY_ROOT/analysis.db "SELECT COUNT(*) FROM telemetry_events;")
  $ echo "Events after restart: $AFTER"
  $ [ "$BEFORE" -eq "$AFTER" ] && echo "✓ IDEMPOTENT" || echo "✗ DUPLICATES"

Expected:
  ✓ IDEMPOTENT
